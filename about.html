About Atvell Trading

Established in 1968, Atvell Trading Sdn Bhd has grown into a recognised name in souvenirs, cultural items and god statues, serving both retail and wholesale customers with reliability and quality for over five decades. Our long-standing presence reflects our commitment to craftsmanship, authenticity and trust across generations.

We specialise in a wide selection of souvenirs for temples, events and gift shops, offering god statues, religious artefacts, cultural decor pieces, festival gifts and customised souvenirs designed to suit a variety of needs and occasions. Our products are supplied to temples, event organisers, retail outlets and collectors, and are often chosen for worship, gifting, display and celebration.

Backed by years of experience, we focus on delivering quality products at fair prices, ensuring dependable supply for bulk orders as well as individual purchases. Whether for offerings, gifts or retail display, Atvell Trading stands for quality, trust and heritage since 1968.
