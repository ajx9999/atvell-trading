About Atvell Trading

Established in 1968, Atvell Trading Sdn Bhd has grown into a recognised name in souvenirs, cultural items and god statues, serving both retail and wholesale customers with reliability and quality for over five decades.

We specialise in souvenirs for temples, events, and gift shops, offering god statues, religious artefacts, cultural decor pieces, festival gifts and customised souvenirs designed to suit a variety of needs and occasions.

Backed by years of experience, we focus on delivering quality products at fair prices, ensuring dependable supply for bulk orders as well as individual purchases. Atvell Trading stands for quality, trust and heritage since 1968.
